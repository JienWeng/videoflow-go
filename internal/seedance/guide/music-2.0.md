## Music and sound (Seedance 2.0 series)

- Symbols tell the model what kind of sound a phrase is. Music goes in round brackets: `(fast-paced rock music is playing in the background)`. A sound effect goes in angle brackets: `< dog barking can be heard in the distance >`.
- With music: one bracketed music phrase describing instrument, tempo and mood, kept quiet under any voice, fading out at the end.
- Without music say so plainly: "No BGM; generate only voices, environmental sounds and action sounds."
- Add one or two fitting sound effects or an ambience per shot where they help ("< a low mechanical hum >"); do not crowd the voice.
