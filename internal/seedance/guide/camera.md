## Camera

- Standard terms can be written directly. Shot size: extreme wide shot, wide shot, medium shot, medium close-up, close-up. Movement: push in, pull out, pan, track, follow, orbit, tilt up, pull back, fixed shot, handheld. Angle: low angle, overhead shot, eye level, over-the-shoulder, first-person perspective.
- Specify only ONE type of camera movement in a single shot. Asking for push, pull, pan and move together makes the image unstable.
- Common techniques also work by name: one-shot / long take, dolly zoom, aerial perspective, FPV, bullet time, speed ramp.
- For niche or technical terms write [term + plain explanation]: "Rack focus: the focus shifts smoothly; the trees in the foreground blur while the person behind them becomes clear."
- Prefer a calm camera (fixed, slow push-in, smooth tracking) when people speak, so faces and lips stay stable.
