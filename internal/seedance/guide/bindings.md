## Binding the connected things

The clip is generated from things connected to the scene: its cast, its places and props, and a storyboard picture sent as the first frame.

- Name each connected character, place and prop once near the top with 2-3 clear and stable static features (clothing, hair, build, material, colour) so it can be uniquely identified, then use exactly that name every time. Keep descriptions concise and free of contradictions.
- The first-frame picture already shows the opening composition. Do not describe it again in detail; continue from it. The first shot must start from what the first frame shows.
- Do not mention a connected character who does not appear in this clip.
- Prefer letting the pictures carry spatial relationships; do not write long text about where things stand.
