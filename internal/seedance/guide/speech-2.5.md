## Spoken lines (Seedance 2.5 series)

- Write each line in the shot where it is spoken, in the form `Character's line (emotion): "content"` or `Dialogue (speaker): "content"`. Examples: `Granny Zhou's line (voice trembling): "太像了。"`, `Dialogue (elderly woman): "Fly safe, my child. Come back to me."`, `Narrator's line (calm, low, sorrowful): "..."`.
- When different lines need different performances, give each its own line in that form with its own emotion.
- Do NOT repeat a line's words after it, and do not attach separate tone, facial-expression or action instructions to specific words of a line. Both trigger unwanted subtitles.
- Describe the voice once per speaker in the bindings: timbre, age, warmth, pace ("a low, warm, slightly rough middle-aged male voice, unhurried"). Seedance 2.5 generates natively in more than 10 languages; keep one language for all lines and name it.
- Write every line exactly once, word for word as given. Never paraphrase, translate or shorten it.
- Leave room: a relaxed pace, small pauses, and the last line finished before the final second.
- For a narrator nobody on screen moves their lips. For characters talking, the speaker is visible and their lips match the words.
