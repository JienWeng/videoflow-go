## Music and sound (Seedance 2.5 series)

- With music: describe it in a sentence (instrument, tempo, mood), say it stays under the voice, and where it swells or fades: "Background music: slow sparse piano with low strings, quiet under the narration, fading out at the end."
- Without music the model may still turn sound effects into background music. State that only voices and natural sound remain and enumerate the terms so no wording slips through: "No music of any kind: no music, background music, BGM, score, instrumental, melody, synth effects or ambient pad." Repeat this audio constraint at both the beginning and the end of the prompt.
- Describe the natural sound of each shot (wind, footsteps, a low hum) so the audio has something real to be.
