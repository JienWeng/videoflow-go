## Constraints and known issues

Constraint words are important: they prevent visual flaws and keep the result stable. Put them last.

- Subtitles appear easily, especially in vertical video and when lines are repeated. Always end with: "Keep it subtitle-free; avoid generating any text or subtitles. Do not generate a logo. Do not generate a watermark." (Skip the subtitle part only if the facts ask for on-screen text.)
- Add stability words: "faces stay stable without deformation; movements are natural and smooth, with no stutter or flicker."
- Add image-quality words that suit the look: "HD, rich details, cinematic texture, natural colours, soft lighting."
- For realistic human characters with strong emotion add "normal human eyes; no glowing eyes". Do not add it for non-human characters whose eyes are described as unusual.
