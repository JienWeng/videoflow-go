# Scene render recovery

The reported scene had a storyboard older than its shot plan. Its stale-input rejection was valid.

Explicit **Update storyboard & render** now runs a tracked operation from chat, Scene Studio, and Create story. It replaces missing/outdated scene storyboards using the current shots, retains old assets, rebuilds the validated render request, and queues video only after preparation succeeds. Matching storyboards are reused. Image/video generation may consume provider or native-account credits; controls disclose this before submission. Nothing generates on page load. Shot-level rendering retains its existing behavior.
